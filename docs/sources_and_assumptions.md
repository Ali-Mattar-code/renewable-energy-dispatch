# Sources and assumptions

Accessed 6 September 2026 unless noted otherwise.

| Input | Reference assumption | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Grid import | AED 0.44/kWh | DEWA industrial high slab of AED 0.38/kWh plus September 2026 fuel surcharge of AED 0.06/kWh; VAT excluded |
| Solar CAPEX | AED 2,500/kW | Rounded screening value close to IRENA’s 2024 global utility-scale installed-cost average of USD 691/kW converted at the AED peg |
| Battery energy CAPEX | AED 900/kWh | Installed screening assumption above IRENA’s reported 2024 utility-scale battery cost of USD 192/kWh to allow for integration/BOS uncertainty |
| Biogas methane fraction | 60% | Within EPA’s reported 50–75% range |
| Biogas electrical efficiency | 35% | Conservative relative to EPA’s statement that common engine-generator electrical efficiency approaches 40% |
| Biogas yield | 60 Nm³/tonne wet feedstock | Illustrative only; deliberately exposed to sensitivity analysis and must be replaced by measured BMP/pilot data |
| Feedstock | 34.5 tonnes/day | Author’s legacy study |
| Load | 9,000 kWh/day | Author’s legacy study |

## Important interpretation

These sources bound general screening assumptions; they do not validate the site. Before investment, replace every reference input with site load data, measured solar resource, geotechnical/electrical studies, a feedstock supply agreement, biochemical methane-potential testing, interconnection terms and vendor quotations.
